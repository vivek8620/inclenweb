Research Project

Development and evaluation of effectiveness of improved take home foods in combination with behaviour change intervention to improve nutritional status in children (NECCTAR Trial)

Principal Investigator

Dr Manoj Kumar Das

Funder

Indian Council of Medical Research

Study Period

48 months

Study Sites

India, 6 sites (Nuapada District, Odisha)

Summary

The NECCTAR trial evaluates an integrated package of improved take-home foods (THR) and social-behavior change communication (SBCC) for children aged 6-36 months. This cluster randomized study aims to reduce undernutrition, wasting, and stunting while documenting impact on caregiver knowledge and community health practices.
