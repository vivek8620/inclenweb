How we make an impact

Our Approach

INCLEN's policy influence rests on four pillars — generating credible multi-site evidence, building a network of trained researchers, engaging directly with decision-makers, and sustaining long-term surveillance to track impact.

Evidence Generation

Multi-site, collaborative research producing nationally representative data on high-priority health issues.

Capacity Building

Training future leaders in clinical epidemiology and health research who carry evidence into policy roles.

Government Engagement

Direct partnerships with MoHFW, ICMR, and state governments to align research with national health priorities.

Surveillance & Monitoring

SOMAARTH-DDESS provides continuous demographic and health data from over 200,000 people to track policy outcomes.

Research Streams

Thematic Focus

INCLEN's five thematic groups generate evidence that directly informs national programmes and international guidelines.

Child Health

Neurodevelopmental disabilities, low birth weight, pneumonia treatment, and vaccine-preventable disease.

Maternal & Reproductive Health

Evidence for improving maternal care quality, neonatal outcomes, and reproductive health access.

Nutrition & NCDs

Social determinants of undernutrition, childhood obesity, and metabolic syndrome across LMICs.

Mental Health

Digital and primary-care-based screening and management for depression, anxiety, and alcohol use.

Injuries & Violence

Multi-country data on childhood injuries to support prevention policies and health system responses.

Track Record

Selected research studies that have directly informed national programmes and government decision-making.

2019Neonatal Health

Management of Possible Serious Bacterial Infection (PSBI)

Implementation research in Palwal, Haryana on managing newborn sepsis where hospital referral is not feasible — directly informing frontline health worker protocols.

2018Vaccines

Rollout of Rotavirus Vaccine & Active AEFI Surveillance

Multi-centre surveillance of adverse events following immunisation (MAASS-India) and post-introduction evaluation of rotavirus vaccine to support national immunisation policy.

Task Force on Childhood Obesity

Nationally coordinated evidence synthesis used to develop India\'s policy framework for prevention and management of childhood and adolescent obesity.

2014Neurodevelopment

Neurodevelopmental Disabilities study (NDD-India)

Landmark multi-site study establishing prevalence of neurodevelopmental disabilities among Indian children — a critical evidence base for national disability policy.

2009Immunisation

Evaluation of Integrated Management of Neonatal & Childhood Illness (IMNCI)

Comprehensive programme evaluation informing India\'s IMNCI scale-up strategy and influencing WHO guidelines for LMIC settings.

2005Universal Immunisation

Evaluation of Universal Immunization Program (UIP)

Multi-site evaluation of India\'s UIP, alongside repeated pulse polio programme evaluations from 1998 onwards, directly shaping national immunisation strategy.
